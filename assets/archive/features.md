# 42 Studenti — What the app can do

Welcome to the **42 Roma** student community portal. Here's everything you can
do inside it.

## Access
- Sign in with your **42 account (OAuth2)** — no passwords to manage.
- **Only students of the 42 Roma campus** can get in.
- No roles and no admins: everyone is an equal student.

## Board (Bacheca)
- Post **messages** with a category: Study, Looking for a team, Casual event,
  Roman life, Market, Off-topic.
- Give posts a **"Daje!"** (like) and leave **comments**.
- **Reply to comments**: a reply automatically references the person.
- You can **edit or delete only your own** posts and comments.
- Feed with **infinite scroll** and **real-time** updates (WebSocket): posts,
  likes and comments appear without reloading.

## Community (unofficial events)
- Create a **poll** ("who's in?") with an **attendance list** and limited spots.
- The **Community** tab gathers all the campus polls.
- Whoever opens the poll sets the capacity; the server won't let more people in
  than allowed.

## Events
- The **official 42 Roma events**, pulled live from the 42 Intra.
- Mark **"I'm interested"**: you're actually signed up on 42.

## Learning materials (Materiale)
- Repos, links and resources for the projects, stored on **GitHub** under
  `assets/material/` (e.g. `assets/material/exam02/help.md`).
- Shown right here: **text, images and PDFs** rendered in the page, and
  **every file is downloadable**. There's also **search** by name or folder.

## Archive (Archivio)
- Campus materials and announcements, stored on GitHub under
  `assets/archive/` — just like this file.

## Extras
- Two **90s retro themes**: Classico Romano and SPQR Hacker.
- Language **Italian / English**, switchable on the fly in Settings.
- **Clubs** and **Staff**: coming soon.

> Want to add material or resources? Open a Pull Request on GitHub inside
> `assets/material/` or `assets/archive/` and it'll show up here.
